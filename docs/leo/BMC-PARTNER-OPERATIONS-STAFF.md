# BMC Partner Operations — Staff Guidance

**Organisation:** Bridgefort Homes Development Ltd  
**Audience:** Staff only  
**Allowed roles:** admin, admin_legal, admin_sales, admin_it  
**Status:** Draft for departmental approval  
**Review date:** 20 November 2026

## Purpose

This guide supports authorised staff administering the Bridgefort Multi-Business Consultants programme across Bridgefort Homes, Bridgefort Travels and Bridgefort Agrovest. It is internal operating guidance, not a substitute for the approved BMC Partner Agreement, BMC Privacy Notice, company policy or applicable law.

## Application lifecycle

1. Receive the BMC application and verify that the applicant accepted the BMC Partner Agreement and acknowledged the BMC Privacy Notice.
2. Confirm the requested divisions and required identity/business verification information.
3. Review each division independently. Use the Admin Console BMC Division Applications queue and record a decision for each division.
4. Approve, reject, suspend or reopen only where the reviewer has the appropriate approval permission and a documented basis.
5. Do not treat account creation, email verification, a referral code, QR code or application submission as approval.
6. Communicate the outcome through approved channels without disclosing internal notes or other partners' records.

## Division permissions

- Homes approval grants only the BMC Homes division entitlement. It does not enrol the partner in the legacy paid BH-Realtors membership, activate a legacy Realtor package, or unlock BH-Realtors commission privileges.
- Travels approval does not confer Homes or BH-Realtors permissions.
- Agrovest approval does not confer Homes or BH-Realtors permissions.
- Approved BMC Homes, Travels and Agrovest applications receive separate division referral codes. Homes referral attribution is recorded on referred-user signup; Travels attribution is recorded on travel bookings; Agrovest attribution is recorded on Agrovest-only orders.
- Existing BH-Realtors members retain their original Homes status, paid membership, referral code and sponsor relationships. Do not merge their legacy identity with the new BMC division code.
- Approval in one division must never be used as evidence of approval in another division.
- Existing BH-Realtors members are retained as legacy Homes members; their referral codes, sponsor relationships and valid historical attribution must not be overwritten during transition.
- New BMC applications remain pending until reviewed. Staff must not manually set profile membership or package fields to bypass the protected approval process.

## Referral and commission integrity

A referral code is an attribution identifier, not proof of employment, official approval, authority to bind the Company or an unconditional right to commission. Homes continues to use the existing BH-Realtors referral system. Approved Travels and Agrovest applications receive separate division-specific links from the BMC Partner Hub; the system disables those codes when the relevant division is no longer approved. Travel booking and Agrovest-only order attribution is recorded separately and is not a commission calculation. Confirm the authoritative referral record, division status and current written commission schedule before answering a claim. Do not invent or promise earnings. Do not manually alter sponsor relationships to favour a partner. Escalate disputed attribution to the responsible manager and preserve relevant audit records.

## Customer funds and payout security

BMCs must not collect customer funds into personal accounts on behalf of Bridgefort unless separately authorised in writing. Changes to partner bank details require re-authentication and independent confirmation using an established contact channel. Never request a partner's bank OTP, ATM PIN, card security code or online banking password. Suspected diversion, impersonation or payout fraud must be escalated immediately to Finance, Compliance and IT as appropriate.

## Privacy and restricted records

Identity documents, contact information, bank details, application records, review notes, referral histories and commission records are restricted. Access must be limited to staff with a legitimate role-based need. Do not place private client/partner records, credentials, API keys or secrets into Leo's knowledge base. Use approved systems for case-specific information and follow retention and incident-response requirements.

## Escalation matrix

- **Division application or approval dispute:** authorised BMC approver / relevant division manager.
- **Agreement interpretation or partner misconduct:** Legal / Compliance.
- **Privacy request or suspected personal-data incident:** Data Protection Officer at dpo@bridgeforthomes.com.
- **Referral attribution or commission dispute:** Finance / BH-Realtors programme owner / relevant division manager.
- **Account compromise, access control or technical incident:** IT.
- **Customer complaint or diverted customer funds:** Customer Retention and Relationship, Finance and Compliance as appropriate.

## Leo restrictions

Leo may use this document only in an authenticated staff context whose permitted roles include the roles above. Leo must not disclose the existence or contents of staff-only application notes, identity checks, bank details, review history, fraud indicators or internal escalation discussions to public users or partners. When the authenticated role or source data is insufficient, Leo must refuse to infer a status and direct the staff member to the Admin Console or responsible department.
