## What is the BMC programme?

Bridgefort Multi-Business Consultants (BMC) is a partner programme through which approved independent consultants introduce prospective customers and business opportunities to participating Bridgefort business divisions. The programme provides one partner identity with separate division applications and approval decisions.

## Participating divisions

- **Bridgefort Homes:** Real estate, property sales and related property services.
- **Bridgefort Travels:** Travel, flights, accommodation, tours and related travel services.
- **Bridgefort Agrovest:** Agriculture, agribusiness and related products or services.

A person may apply for one or more divisions. Each selected division is reviewed independently. Approval in one division does not automatically approve another.

## Applying to become a BMC

Applicants complete the BMC registration process, provide accurate information, select the divisions they wish to apply for, and accept the BMC Partner Agreement and acknowledge the separate BMC Privacy Notice. The Company may request identity or business verification and additional information where reasonably required.

Submitting an application does not itself make the applicant an approved BMC, employee, agent, franchisee or authorised representative of Bridgefort. The applicant must wait for formal approval before representing themselves as an approved partner or carrying out division-specific partner activities.

## Partner responsibilities

Approved BMCs must communicate honestly, use approved marketing materials, protect customer and Company information, follow division-specific rules, and avoid misleading claims or unauthorised promises. BMCs must not sign contracts, bind the Company, alter prices, promise discounts, guarantee investment returns or collect customer funds into personal accounts unless specifically authorised in writing.

## Referral identity

A referral code or link is used for attribution. It is not proof of employment, partner approval, commission entitlement or authority to represent Bridgefort. Referral activity and commission eligibility depend on the applicable division rules, verified attribution and completed transaction conditions. Existing BH-Realtors referral codes and sponsor relationships are intended to remain preserved during the programme transition.

## Commissions

Commission rates, qualifying events, payment timing and any applicable conditions are set out in the current approved written schedule for the relevant division. A lead, enquiry, booking request or referral alone does not guarantee payment. Leo must not quote rates or promise earnings unless the current approved schedule is available in the authorised knowledge base.

## Privacy and information security

The BMC Privacy Notice explains how Bridgefort handles BMC applicant and partner information, including contact details, verification documents, division applications, referral records and commission-related information. Marketing consent is optional and separate from application acceptance. Bridgefort does not ask partners for their bank OTP, ATM PIN, card security code or online banking password.

## Which terms apply?

The BMC Partner Agreement and BMC Privacy Notice are separate documents created specifically for the BMC partner relationship. They do not replace, amend or merge with Bridgefort's general Terms of Service and general Privacy Policy, which continue to apply to general services and client relationships.
