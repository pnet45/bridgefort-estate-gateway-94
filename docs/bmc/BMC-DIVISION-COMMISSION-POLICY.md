# Bridgefort BMC Division Commission Policy

**Company:** Bridgefort Homes Development Ltd  
**Programme:** Bridgefort Multi-Business Consultants (BMC)  
**Status:** Management-approved policy; operational activation is subject to release of the matching commission engine.

## 1. Independent division schedules

BMC commissions are calculated and reported independently for each business division.

| Division | Commission | Attribution |
|---|---:|---|
| Bridgefort Homes | Existing BH-Realtors schedule | Existing Homes rules remain unchanged |
| Bridgefort Agrovest | 10% of each cleared customer instalment | Direct referral only; no upliner/downline |
| Bridgefort Travels & Tours | 10% of full customer payment received | Direct referral only; no upliner/downline |

## 2. Agrovest

An approved BMC with an approved Agrovest division referral is entitled to 10% of each customer payment received and confirmed by Bridgefort for a qualifying Agrovest-only order. Each cleared instalment is recorded as a separate commission event using its own payment reference; commission is not deferred until the full order balance is paid. No third-party costs are deducted before calculating the commission. The direct referring BMC is the sole beneficiary.

## 3. Travels & Tours

An approved BMC with an approved Travels division referral is entitled to 10% of the full customer payment received by Bridgefort for a qualifying travel transaction. No third-party costs are deducted before calculating the commission. A travel enquiry or booking request alone does not constitute a paid transaction. An authorized manager must confirm the booking and record each cleared customer payment and its unique payment reference before the corresponding 10% commission is earned. Multiple instalments create separate commission events. The direct referring BMC is the sole beneficiary.

## 4. Homes

The existing BH-Realtors commission framework, including its current rates, eligibility, referral relationships and applicable upliner arrangements, remains unchanged. BMC Homes referral attribution does not replace or modify the legacy BH-Realtors commission engine.

## 5. Multi-division participation and controls

A BMC may be approved for one or more divisions. Each transaction must be attributed to its correct division. Referral codes are division-specific. Cross-division attribution, self-referrals, duplicate commissions and unauthorized commission claims are prohibited.

Agrovest and Travels & Tours must never generate recruitment-based, upliner or downline commissions. Their commission records are maintained separately from the BH-Realtors MLM commission ledger and wallet.

## 6. Payment, reversal and payout controls

Commissions are calculated on gross customer payment received, not net profit. A commission is earned only after the applicable customer funds have been confirmed and cleared. A full refund, reversal or chargeback must reverse the corresponding unpaid commission entry. A partial refund or a recovery against an already-paid commission requires a separately recorded Finance-approved adjustment; it must not be silently deducted from another commission or wallet. Payouts require authorized approval and a recorded payout reference.

## 7. Management authority

Bridgefort Homes Development Ltd retains authority to verify referrals and payments, resolve attribution disputes, investigate fraud, correct calculation errors, authorize payouts and amend the schedule through formal management approval.

This policy is specific to BMC commission arrangements and operates alongside the BMC Partner Agreement and BMC Privacy Notice. It does not replace the company's general customer Terms of Service or general Privacy Policy.
