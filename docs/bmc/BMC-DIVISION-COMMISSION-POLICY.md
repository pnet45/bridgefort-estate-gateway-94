# Bridgefort BMC Division Commission Policy

**Company:** Bridgefort Homes Development Ltd  
**Programme:** Bridgefort Multi-Business Consultants (BMC)  
**Status:** Management-approved policy; operational activation is subject to release of the matching commission engine.

## 1. Independent division schedules

BMC commissions are calculated and reported independently for each business division.

| Division | Commission | Attribution |
|---|---:|---|
| Bridgefort Homes land sales (BMC code) | 15% | Direct BMC referral; no BMC upliner commission |
| Bridgefort Homes house sales (BMC code) | 5% | Direct BMC referral; no second-level house-sale commission |
| Bridgefort Agrovest | 10% of each cleared customer instalment | Direct referral only; no upliner/downline |
| Bridgefort Travels & Tours | 10% of full customer payment received | Direct referral only; no upliner/downline |

All published rates are gross commission rates. Applicable withholding tax (WHT) and approved administrative fees are deducted from the commission payable. The approved fee amounts and tax treatment must be disclosed to partners before payout; no unapproved or undisclosed deductions may be applied.

## 2. Agrovest

An approved BMC with an approved Agrovest division referral is entitled to 10% of each customer payment received and confirmed by Bridgefort for a qualifying Agrovest-only order. Each cleared instalment is recorded as a separate commission event using its own payment reference; commission is not deferred until the full order balance is paid. No third-party costs are deducted before calculating the commission. The direct referring BMC is the sole beneficiary.

## 3. Travels & Tours

An approved BMC with an approved Travels division referral is entitled to 10% of the full customer payment received by Bridgefort for a qualifying travel transaction. No third-party costs are deducted before calculating the commission. A travel enquiry or booking request alone does not constitute a paid transaction. An authorized manager must confirm the booking and record each cleared customer payment and its unique payment reference before the corresponding 10% commission is earned. Multiple instalments create separate commission events. The direct referring BMC is the sole beneficiary.

## 4. Homes

For a transaction attributed with a canonical BMC referral code, the direct BMC earns 15% on qualifying land/plot sales and 5% on qualifying house, apartment and other residential sales. BMC Homes commissions are direct-only; no second-level or recruitment-based commission is created. The relevant Homes division must be approved before a commission becomes payable. If a qualifying customer payment clears before approval, the commission is recorded as held and released only after approval. Existing BH-Realtors referral codes remain valid and continue through the legacy BH-Realtors commission rules, including the existing eligible second-level land-sale arrangement. A BMC referral attribution does not create or modify a legacy BH-Realtors sponsor relationship.

## 5. Multi-division participation and controls

A BMC receives one canonical referral code accepted across Homes, Travels and Agrovest. Referral attribution may be recorded while a division is pending approval; commission remains held until that division is approved. Each transaction is attributed to its correct division. Self-referrals, duplicate commissions and unauthorized commission claims are prohibited.

BMC Homes, Agrovest and Travels & Tours commissions are direct-only and are maintained separately from the legacy BH-Realtors MLM commission ledger and wallet. Existing BH-Realtors codes continue to use the legacy MLM rules.

## 6. Payment, reversal and payout controls

Commissions are calculated on the qualifying gross customer payment received, not net profit. Applicable WHT and approved administrative fees are deducted from the gross commission to determine the amount payable. A commission is earned only after the applicable customer funds have been confirmed and cleared. A full refund, reversal or chargeback must reverse the corresponding unpaid commission entry. A partial refund or a recovery against an already-paid commission requires a separately recorded Finance-approved adjustment; it must not be silently deducted from another commission or wallet. Payouts require authorized approval and a recorded payout reference.

## 7. Management authority

Bridgefort Homes Development Ltd retains authority to verify referrals and payments, resolve attribution disputes, investigate fraud, correct calculation errors, authorize payouts and amend the schedule through formal management approval.

This policy is specific to BMC commission arrangements and operates alongside the BMC Partner Agreement and BMC Privacy Notice. It does not replace the company's general customer Terms of Service or general Privacy Policy.
